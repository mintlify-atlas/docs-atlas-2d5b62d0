## Get Started in Minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Purchase and download TanaFlows">
    After purchasing, download the theme `.zip` file from your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the theme to Ghost">
    Go to **Ghost Admin → Settings → Design → Change theme → Advanced → Upload theme** and upload the zip file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the theme">
    Click **Activate** once the upload completes. Your site immediately uses TanaFlows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your site">
    Customize site settings, enable multilingual support, and add comment integrations through **Ghost Admin → Settings → Design → Customize**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
