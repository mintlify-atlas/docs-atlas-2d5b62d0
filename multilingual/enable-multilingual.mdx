TanaFlows includes a professional multilingual feature that requires no modifications to system configuration files or theme code. You activate and configure everything directly inside Ghost Admin through three Custom Settings.

## How the Language Switcher Behaves

Before diving into configuration, it helps to understand what the language switcher does in different contexts:

- **Static pages (Homepage, Blog listing, Category pages)** — the switcher is always visible and clickable. Visitors can freely jump between language editions at any time.
- **Content pages (Posts and custom Pages):**
  - **If a translation exists** (the post has the `#multilingual` tag and a matching slug on the other instance) — the switcher works normally and takes the reader directly to the translated version.
  - **If no translation exists** — the switcher is **grayed out and locked** (unclickable) and shows the label "Not translated." This prevents readers from clicking through to a missing page and hitting a 404 error.

---

## Configuration Steps

All three settings live in **Ghost Admin → Settings → Design → Custom Settings** for the TanaFlows theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Multilingual">
    Find the **Enable Multilingual** toggle and switch it to **On**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Languages">
    In the **Languages** field, enter the list of languages your site supports using this format:

    ```text
    language_code|Display_Name
    ```

    Separate multiple languages with a comma. For example:

    ```text
    vi|Tiếng Việt,en|English
    ```

    <Info>
      The **first language** in the list becomes the **Default Language**. Its URL path will not contain a language prefix. All other languages will be served from a subdirectory matching their `language_code`.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Root URL">
    In the **Root URL** field, enter your main website domain without a trailing slash:

    ```text
    https://tanaflows.com
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
