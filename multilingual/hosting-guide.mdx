Supporting multiple languages in TanaFlows requires running **two separate Ghost instances** on the same domain, with each language served from its own subdirectory path. For example, your primary English site lives at `https://domain.com` while a Vietnamese or German edition lives at `https://domain.com/vi/` or `https://domain.com/de/`. A traffic router (reverse proxy) sits in front of both instances and directs each incoming request to the correct Ghost backend.

## Architecture Overview

The core setup always looks like this:

- **Primary Ghost instance** — handles your root domain (`https://domain.com`)
- **Secondary Ghost instance** — handles a subdirectory path (`https://domain.com/vi/`)
- **Reverse proxy (Nginx)** — routes requests between the two instances based on the URL prefix

Both Ghost sites maintain completely separate admin panels, databases, and content — only the domain is shared.

### Configure Subdirectory Installs on SynapsMedia

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Publications">
    Ensure you have at least two Ghost publications running under your SynapsMedia account — one will be the primary site and the other the secondary language site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Settings">
    In the SynapsMedia Panel, open the settings page of the Ghost site you want to run under a subdirectory (the secondary language site).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Subdirectory Install">
    Scroll down to the **Domains** section and click **Enable subdirectory install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Routing">
    In the **Domain** dropdown, select the main domain of your active primary Ghost site. In the **Subdirectory path** field, enter the desired path — for example `vi` or `de`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Restart">
    Click **Enable Subdirectory Install**. SynapsMedia will automatically reconfigure and restart both publications within about a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
**Rules and Restrictions to Keep in Mind**

- **Separate Ghost Admins** — each site's admin panel stays independent. You can reach the subdirectory admin at `https://domain.com/{subdir}/ghost/`.
- **Email Deliverability** — all publications under the same domain send membership and newsletter emails using the root site's sending domain configuration.
- **Sitemaps & Robots.txt** — sitemaps are separate; submit the subdirectory sitemap to Google Search Console manually. `robots.txt` rules do not merge automatically and must be manually combined on the root publication.
- **Social Web (ActivityPub)** — federation is disabled on subdirectory installations.
</Info>

## Option 2: Self-Host on Railway

Railway is the easiest self-hosted path thanks to an official **1-Click Setup** that provisions the traffic router, two Ghost sites, and a database all at once.

**What you need:**
- A [Railway account](https://railway.app)
- A custom domain (e.g. `your-domain.com`)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch the 1-Click Setup">
    Click the Deploy on Railway button to start. Railway will automatically provision:

    - **Traffic Router** (Nginx Proxy)
    - **Primary Blog** (e.g. English)
    - **Secondary Blog** (e.g. Vietnamese)
    - **Database** (MySQL)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Private Links">
    After deployment, go to the **Settings** tab of each Blog service on Railway. Scroll to the **Networking** section and copy the private link ending in `.railway.internal` — for example:

    ```text
    http://ghost.railway.internal:2368
    http://ghost-vi.railway.internal:2368
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Traffic Router">
    Select the **Nginx Proxy** service, open its **Variables** tab, and set the following:

    | Variable | Value |
    |---|---|
    | `SERVER_NAME` | Your domain, e.g. `your-domain.com` |
    | `GHOST_ROOT_URL` | Private link of your Primary Blog |
    | `GHOST_INSTANCES` | Path + private link of your Secondary Blog, e.g. `/vi:http://ghost-vi.railway.internal:2368` |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Public Addresses">
    Update the `url` variable on each Ghost service:

    - **Primary Blog** → `https://your-domain.com`
    - **Secondary Blog** → `https://your-domain.com/vi/`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Your Domain">
    Open the **Nginx Proxy** service settings. Under **Networking**, click **+ Add Domain**, enter your domain, and connect it to the proxy on port `8080`. Then point your DNS records to Railway at your domain registrar.

    Once active:
    - `https://your-domain.com` loads the **primary blog**
    - `https://your-domain.com/vi/` loads the **secondary language blog**
  </Step>
</Steps>
