---
title: "Setting Up Cusdis Comments in TanaFlows Ghost Theme"
sidebarTitle: "Cusdis"
description: "Add privacy-first, open-source Cusdis comments to your TanaFlows Ghost theme using your site's data-app-id from the Cusdis dashboard."
---

[Cusdis](https://cusdis.com/) is an open-source comment system with a free hosted tier. Here's how to configure it for the TanaFlows theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Cusdis account and get your site ID">
    [Create a new Cusdis account](https://cusdis.com/api/auth/signin). After logging in, add a new site.

    Once your site is created, click the **Embedded code** button to view your embed snippet. Locate and copy the `data-app-id` value from that snippet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste data-app-id into Ghost Admin">
    Go to **Settings** → **Design** → **Post**, select **Cusdis** as the comment provider, and paste your `data-app-id` in the **3rd comment id** field.
  </Step>
</Steps>
