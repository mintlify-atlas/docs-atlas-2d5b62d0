---
title: "Enable Ghost Native Comments in TanaFlows Ghost Theme"
sidebarTitle: "Ghost Comments"
description: "Enable Ghost's native built-in comment system on your TanaFlows site — no third-party service required, just a quick toggle in Ghost settings."
---

Ghost ships with a built-in comment system that lets your members leave comments directly on your posts without any third-party service. Because comments are tied to Ghost membership, only people with an account on your site can participate — which naturally keeps quality high and spam low. This is the simplest option to get started with if your publication already uses Ghost's membership features.

<Note>
  Ghost's built-in commenting is available on **Ghost Pro Creator plans and above**, as well as self-hosted Ghost installations. If you are on the Starter plan, you will need to upgrade or use a third-party comment provider instead.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable comments in Ghost Membership settings">
    In your Ghost Admin dashboard, go to **Settings** → **Membership**.

    Scroll down to find the **Commenting** toggle and switch it on. This activates Ghost's native comment system across all posts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Ghost as the comment provider in the Customizer">
    Go to **Settings** → **Design & branding** → **Customizer** → **Post** → **Comment provider** and choose **Ghost**.

    No additional IDs or configuration strings are required — Ghost handles everything automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Click **Save** in the Customizer, then open any post on your site. Members who are signed in will see the comment box at the bottom of each post. Visitors who are not signed in will see a prompt to sign up or log in before commenting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Ghost comments inherit your site's branding and respect your membership tiers. You can manage and moderate comments directly from the Ghost Admin dashboard under each individual post.
</Tip>
