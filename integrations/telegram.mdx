---
title: "Setting Up Telegram Comments in TanaFlows Ghost Theme"
sidebarTitle: "Telegram"
description: "Connect Telegram's comment widget to TanaFlows using comments.app — great for communities already active on Telegram, using your Site ID."
---

The [Telegram Comments widget](https://comments.app/) brings Telegram-powered discussions to your Ghost blog posts. The interface is very friendly, although it lacks some notification and comment moderation features, making it best suited for blogs with an audience already active on Telegram.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the site to the comment app">
    Go to [comments.app](https://comments.app/) to connect your site (requires a Telegram account). Fill in your site information and domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy your Site ID">
    After registering your site, copy the **Site ID** displayed for your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste your Site ID into Ghost Admin">
    Go to **Settings** → **Design** → **Post**, paste your **Site ID** in the **3rd comment id** field, and select **Telegram** as the comment provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>
