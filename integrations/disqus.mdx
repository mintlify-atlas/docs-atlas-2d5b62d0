[Disqus](https://disqus.com/) is one of the most widely used third-party commenting platforms on the web. It gives site owners a full-featured comment section with moderation tools, spam filtering, and a familiar interface that many readers already have an account with. Disqus is a practical choice if you want to get a comment system running with minimal configuration and prefer a hosted solution that requires no server management.

## Prerequisites

- A [Disqus account](https://disqus.com/profile/signup/)
- A Disqus **site** created for your Ghost publication (this generates your shortname)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Disqus account and register your site">
    [Sign up for a Disqus account](https://disqus.com/profile/signup/) if you do not have one already. After logging in, click **Get Started** and choose **I want to install Disqus on my site**.

    Fill in your **Website Name**. Disqus will automatically generate a subdomain from this name — this subdomain is your **shortname**.

    <Note>
      Subdomain = Website Name = Shortname. For example, if you name your site `ghostfam`, your shortname is `ghostfam` and your Disqus URL will be `ghostfam.disqus.com`.
    </Note>

    Complete the setup flow and note your shortname — you will need it in the next step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Disqus as the comment provider in Ghost">
    In your Ghost Admin dashboard, go to **Settings** → **Design** → **Customizer** → **Post** → **Comment provider** and choose **Disqus**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste your shortname into the Comment ID field">
    In the **3rd comment id** field, enter your Disqus shortname.

    For example, if your shortname is `ghostfam`, enter:

    ```
    ghostfam
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Click **Save** in the Customizer, then open any post on your site. The Disqus comment thread should appear at the bottom of each post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you change your Disqus site name after setup, remember to update the shortname in the Ghost Customizer as well — otherwise comments will fail to load.
</Tip>
