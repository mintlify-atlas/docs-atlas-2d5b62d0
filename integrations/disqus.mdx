---
title: "Setting Up Disqus Comments in TanaFlows Ghost Theme"
sidebarTitle: "Disqus"
description: "Add Disqus to your TanaFlows Ghost site for a widely recognized comment platform with built-in moderation, reactions, and spam filtering."
---

[Disqus](https://disqus.com/) is a popular and widely used commenting platform that allows website owners to integrate a comment system easily. It provides a friendly interface for both site owners and users, making it simple to manage and moderate comments. Here's how to integrate Disqus into the TanaFlows theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Disqus account and get the shortname">
    [Sign up for a Disqus account](https://disqus.com/profile/signup/). After logging in, create a new site.

    In the website name field, give your site a name and Disqus will create a subdomain for you.

    <Note>
      Subdomain = Website Name = Shortname. For example, if you name your site `ghostfam`, your shortname is `ghostfam`.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the shortname into Ghost Admin">
    Go to **Settings** → **Design** → **Post**, paste your `shortname` in the **3rd comment id** field, and select **Disqus** as the comment provider. The demo below has the shortname `ghostfam`.

    ```text
    ghostfam
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
