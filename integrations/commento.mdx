title: "Setting Up Commento Comments in TanaFlows Ghost Theme"
sidebarTitle: "Commento"
description: "Integrate Commento's fast, privacy-focused comment system into TanaFlows — supports both the hosted service and a self-hosted Commento instance."
---

[Commento](https://commento.io/) is a web-based commenting system that focuses on speed and privacy. It offers a clean interface, is compatible with Ghost CMS, and is available as a paid hosted service (starting at $10/month) or as a free self-hosted solution.

<Note>
  Commento offers a self-hosted version, allowing you to host the commenting system on your own server. This can be a cost-effective solution, as it eliminates the need for third-party hosting services.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your domain in the Commento dashboard">
    At the Commento dashboard, add your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Commento as the comment provider in Ghost">
    Go to **Settings** → **Design & branding** → **Customize** → **Post** and choose **Commento** as the comment provider.

    - If you use the hosted Commento service, leave the **3rd comment id** field **empty**.
    - If you are using a self-hosted Commento instance, paste your **custom Commento domain** in the **3rd comment id** field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Commento will automatically load the comment count on post cards and single post pages.
