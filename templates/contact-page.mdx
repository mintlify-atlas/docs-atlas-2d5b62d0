TanaFlows includes a ready-made **Contact** page template that wires a spam-protected contact form into your Ghost site without requiring any custom code. The form is powered by [Formspark](https://formspark.io/) — a lightweight form-backend service with a free tier — and is activated by creating a Ghost page with the slug `contact` and pasting your Formspark action URL into Ghost's Design settings. Visitors receive a clean, on-brand form; you receive email notifications with a **Reply-to** header set to the sender's address so you can respond directly from your inbox.

---

## Step 1: Create the Contact Page in Ghost Admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new Page">
    Go to **Ghost Admin → Pages → New Page**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the page settings">
    Open page settings (gear icon ⚙️) and set:
    - **Page URL (Slug):** `contact` — this exact slug activates the contact template.
    - **Template:** `Default` (the TanaFlows theme detects the `contact` slug automatically).
    - **Title:** optional — you can leave it blank or use a headline such as *Get in Touch*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add page content">
    Write a brief description in the page body. This text appears above the form and sets expectations for visitors (response time, what to include in a message, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the page">
    Click **Publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 2: Create a Formspark Form

Formspark provides the form submission endpoint and handles spam filtering, email notifications, and reply routing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register a free account">
    Go to [formspark.io](https://formspark.io/pricing/) and sign up for a free account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new form">
    In the Formspark dashboard, click **New form**. Give it a recognisable name such as `TanaFlows Contact`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the form action URL">
    After creation, Formspark displays your unique form action URL:

    ```html
    https://submit-form.com/9syxxxx
    ```

    Copy this URL — you will paste it into Ghost in the next step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 3: Connect the Form to Ghost

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ghost Design settings">
    In Ghost Admin, go to **Settings → Design → Site-wide**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the form action URL">
    Find the **Contact form** field and paste your Formspark action URL (e.g. `https://submit-form.com/9syxxxx`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The contact form at `yourdomain.com/contact/` is now live and submissions will route to your Formspark account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Contact Messages

Formspark sends an email notification to your registered address each time the form is submitted. The email's **Reply-to** header is automatically set to the sender's email address, so you can reply directly from your mail client without copying addresses manually.

<Note>
  To configure where notification emails are delivered, open the Formspark dashboard, select your form, and update the **Notification email** under form settings. You can also enable integrations with Slack, Zapier, or webhooks for more advanced workflows.
</Note>

---

## Anti-Bot Protection

Formspark includes built-in **Botpoison** anti-spam protection at no extra cost. This invisible challenge verifies that submissions come from real humans before they reach your inbox — no CAPTCHA images or puzzles are shown to legitimate visitors.

<Tip>
  If you use Formspark's free plan, keep an eye on your monthly submission quota. Upgrading to a paid plan removes submission limits and unlocks additional integrations such as custom redirect URLs and file uploads.
</Tip>
